## Writing a post

Drop an `.mdx` file into `content/blog`. The filename is the slug.

```mdx
---
title: My post
date: 2026-11-25
---

Text, **bold**, [links](https://maty.as) and `code`.
```

Add `draft: true` to the frontmatter to hide a post.
